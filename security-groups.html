1. Roles

The 6-tier role enum. No inheritance — every grant below is explicit. Roles themselves aren't editable in this sandbox; only permission grants and security groups are.

RoleRankDescription
Manager 1 Full access. Manages people and access groups, undoes changes, deletes records, approves quotes, and sees every report.
Supervisor 2 Runs the day-to-day. Views and edits records, runs and confirms imports, manages workers, licences, rosters and dispatch, creates quotes, and sees cost, margin, rates and supplier logins. Cannot manage user accounts, open reports, undo changes, or delete records.
Employee 3 Everyday staff access. Views records, quotes, maintenance, equipment and field information, and can start an import. Cannot see personal details, hours, licences, cost or margin.
Apprentice 4 Read-only across records, maintenance, equipment and field information. Can see imports but not start one, and cannot view quotes.
Labour Hire 5 Agency worker. Sees field information and the equipment list, nothing else.
Subcontractor 6 External trade engaged for a job, not an agency worker. Sees field information only.

2. Permission matrix

Every permission key, grouped by area, with which roles hold it. ✓ = granted. Toggle a box to change a grant, or use the row's × to remove that permission entirely.

3. All permissions

Every permission key in one flat table, independent of area grouping, reflecting your current sandbox state. Click a column header to sort; click again to reverse.

4. Default security groups

Named bundles of extra permission keys, additive on top of a user's base role — not a role themselves. A fresh tenant starts with zero groups; these are the canonical starter templates eq-shell seeds on tenant creation. Edit a card's name, description, or grants, remove one, or add a new bundle below.

Scope of this document: the canonical model only — the 6 roles, the full permission matrix, and the default security-group templates, as defined in eq-roles. Changes you make on this page are local to your browser and exploratory only; they do not touch live, per-tenant group membership or tenant-specific (non-default) groups — those are created and managed per tenant in eq-shell's Access Control page (admin.manage_groups).
